Rules & Guidelines

Terms and conditions governing Prompt to Product – AI Web Challenge

1. Eligibility & Team Formation

  • The hackathon is open to students, developers, and independent creators across India.
  • Teams must consist of 1 to 4 members (solo participants or teams up to 4).
  • The team leader creates a single account for the team and handles project submission. Additional members do not need to register separate portal accounts.
  • A participant may only be a registered member of one team. Multi-team memberships will result in disqualification.

2. AI Tools & Prompt Engineering Policy

  • The use of AI coding assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Cursor, GitHub Copilot) is enthusiastically encouraged.
  • Teams must demonstrate thoughtful prompt orchestration, system design, and functional refinement. Simply copy-pasting broken AI hallucinated code will be penalized in code quality.
  • All third-party APIs used must be legally acquired and compliant with provider terms.

3. Submission Deliverables & Format

  • The portal accepts ONLY a live deployed public website URL (HTTP/HTTPS).
  • Do NOT upload ZIP files, PDF documents, or video files to this portal.
  • The deployed application must remain publicly accessible without requiring credentials through the entire judging period.
  • Teams must also submit on the official Unstop opportunity page to ensure institutional compliance.

4. Disqualification Criteria

  • Submitting non-functional or unreachable links (such as http://localhost:3000).
  • Submitting malicious software, phishing pages, or offensive materials.
  • Plagiarism or misrepresenting existing commercial products as original challenge submissions.
  • Violating the submission deadline without explicit prior administrator extension.

5. Evaluation & Jury Decisions

  • Submissions are evaluated across 6 categories for a total of 60 marks.
  • Decisions made by the technical evaluation jury appointed by SSRS Group of Companies are final and binding.
  • Top 3 teams will receive official Certificates of Excellence. Verified participants submitting compliant projects receive participation certificates.
