About Prompt to Product

AI Web Challenge organized by SSRS Group of Companies

SSRS Group of Companies

Official Organizer

SSRS Group of Companies is committed to fostering practical technological excellence, nurturing youth capability, and connecting aspiring student engineers with industry-grade software methodologies.

The Prompt to Product – AI Web Challenge was conceptualized to test modern software building instincts. With generative AI rapidly redefining web development, knowing how to formulate clear architectural prompts, evaluate AI-generated code, and deploy functional applications in record time has become a superpower.

Challenge Summary

Event Name: Prompt to Product – AI Web Challenge

Organizer: SSRS Group of Companies

Format: Online Hackathon

Team Size: 1 to 4 Members

Domain: AI & Web Development

Submission Type: Deployed Website URL Only

Awards & Recognition

This hackathon focuses on genuine technical merit and institutional recognition. There are no cash prizes. Instead, high-achieving teams receive prestigious institutional accolades:

  • Certificates of Excellence for the Top 3 ranked teams.
  • Official Participation Certificates for all verified teams that submit a working deployed application.
  • Special highlight across SSRS Group of Companies media networks.
