About The Challenge

Empowering Builders with Generative AI

Prompt to Product – AI Web Challenge is organized by SSRS Group of Companies to bridge the gap between creative prompt ideas and production-ready digital applications.

Unlike traditional coding competitions where participants spend days writing boilerplate code, this challenge emphasizes system thinking, intelligent UX, prompt orchestration, and fast deployment.

Rubric

Evaluation Criteria

Projects are evaluated out of 60 marks across six core dimensions.

Functionality & Execution

10 Marks

Reliable operation, smooth user journeys, and absence of fatal runtime errors or broken user flows.

AI & Prompt Engineering

10 Marks

Depth and creativity in integrating generative AI, LLM APIs, prompt chaining, or intelligent reasoning.

UI / UX & Responsiveness

10 Marks

Visual elegance, typography, accessible color contrast, and seamless responsiveness across devices.

Real-World Impact

10 Marks

Clear utility solving an authentic business, community, productivity, or consumer pain point.

Code & Architecture Quality

10 Marks

Clean project organization, maintainable component design, and secure handling of credentials.

Live Deployment Quality

10 Marks

Fast load speed, HTTPS security, valid domain hosting, and immediate accessibility for reviewers.

Specifications

Submission Requirements

Read carefully before deploying and submitting your application.

What is Required

  • ✓ A live, publicly accessible website URL (e.g. https://your-app.vercel.app).
  • ✓ Working interactive prototype responsive on mobile and desktop.
  • ✓ Demonstrable AI feature, generative component, or prompt workflow.
  • ✓ Complete parallel submission on the official Unstop opportunity page.

What is Strictly Disallowed

  • ✗ No ZIP file uploads, PDF project reports, or video attachments on this portal.
  • ✗ No localhost links (e.g. http://localhost:3000).
  • ✗ No non-functional mockups or static landing pages without working features.
  • ✗ No duplicate team registrations or member sharing across teams.
